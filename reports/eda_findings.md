## Operating-Regime Findings

Initial current-based regime analysis shows a strong change in
equipment response with load current.

The median Reference_Parameter increases across the four current
quartiles from approximately 17.54 to 19.42 to 26.92 to 41.38.
Sensor S1, S2 and S3 also increase systematically with current.

This demonstrates that globally unusual sensor/reference values
cannot automatically be treated as abnormal because higher values
may be expected under higher-current operating conditions.

The Invalid proportion is relatively stable across current regimes
(approximately 12–16%), indicating that current regime alone is not
a sufficient validity classifier.

TST-0213 lies in the lowest-current regime but has Sensor_S2 =
-1.5509, far below the historical lower tail for S2 in that regime,
while S1 and S3 remain positive. This makes it a strong candidate
for sensor-consistency investigation.

These results support an anomaly-detection strategy based on
deviation from expected operating behaviour rather than global
thresholds.
